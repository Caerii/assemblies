# Volume 5: Dynamics And Statistical Mechanics

This volume turns the first four volumes into measured dynamics.

The core question is: when do sparse winner sets behave like stable
macrostates, and when do they drift, collapse, or fail to recover?

## Notebooks

- `01_order_parameters_and_regimes.ipynb`: run small projection sweeps and
  inspect order parameters such as density, consecutive overlap, chance
  overlap, stabilization round, and new-winner turnover.

## Future Notebooks

- finite-size scaling for `N`, `K`, `p`, and `beta`
- phase-diagram slices for projection and persistence
- basin-of-attraction maps from perturbed initial states
- turnover, entropy, and drift diagnostics
- analytic mean-field notes paired with simulation checks

These notebooks should be treated as regime probes. They are not replacements
for registered experiments under `research/experiments/`.
