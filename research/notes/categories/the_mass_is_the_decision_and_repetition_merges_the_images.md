# The mass IS the decision — and repetition under scaling merges the label images

**Task #141 (E12). Experiment: `research/experiments/drive_decomposition.py` (bars C1–C3 registered before any mass was read). SLOW-scaled × R ∈ {1,4}, seeds 42–46, n=3000.**

## C1 CONFIRMED, decisively

Per-item afferent mass — the summed weight from a PL form's core-assembly
rows into the PL-image vs SG-image columns of NUMBER — predicts item
correctness at **ρ = 0.846 ± 0.046** (every seed 0.78–0.87; the graded
margin companion reads 0.865; pass−fail separation ~27 weight units).
C2 passes at the bar: "girls", the exposure-escapee, ranks top-2 in 3/5
seeds. The SG default is per-item afferent mass, exactly as E11's
forensics predicted: a form seen ~once wrote marginal PL-column mass,
and when it does not suffice, the probe reads the SG attractor. The
weight readout is WHERE-structured (it sums over exactly the columns the
recall readout compares), which is why it sees the decision that the
all-candidates input_drive probe never could.

## C3 answers "why did repetition fail the scaled arm" — with a mechanism nobody registered

| | R1 | R4 |
|---|---|---|
| mean mass_PL | ref | **+10.8** (rises — repetition writes the mass) |
| mean mass_SG | ref | +1.4 (flat) |
| PL item accuracy | 0.40–0.60 | **0.30–0.50 (WORSE)** |
| **shared SG∩PL image columns (of 30)** | **1–4** | **8–17** |

Reading (a) of the registration — the mass arrives but the ceiling is
downstream — is confirmed, and the guard names the downstream mechanism:
**under repetition + scaling the SG and PL label images MERGE** (up to
17/30 shared columns at R4 vs 1–4 at R1). Repetition multiplies the
label-stimulus projections along with the episodes, and the images
converge onto shared NUMBER neurons — the reinforcement-merges-areas
tradeoff surfacing at the feature-image level. More per-item mass and
less image separability cancel: E8/E9's flat scaled-R4 means and their
seed-bistable variance (merge depth is seed-dependent, 8–17) both fall
out of this one mechanism.

## What the arc now says, end to end

The E-series question — what caps scaled number at 0.65 — resolves as:
**per-item afferent mass, pinned by per-form exposure (~1.5 episodes,
E7's invariant), with the naive exposure lever (phase repetition)
self-defeating because it multiplies label-stim projections and merges
the images.** The lever that remains is REDISTRIBUTION, not
multiplication: a Zipfian allocation of the fixed episode budget raises
per-form exposure for frequent forms while holding the total label-stim
mass constant — no extra image-merging pressure by construction.

**E13 (to register): the synthesis.** Zipfian frame allocation (corpus
side, generation-owned) × slow scaling, with image separation as a
REGISTERED GUARD, not an afterthought: (a) number balanced beats the
0.65 ceiling; (b) shared SG∩PL image columns stay at the R1 floor;
(c) per-item mass delta rises on the frequent forms and accuracy follows
item-by-item (the E12 readout re-run as the mechanism check). If (a)
fails while (c) holds, the residual is the readout; if (b) fails, Zipf
smuggled multiplication in and the design is wrong, not the theory.
