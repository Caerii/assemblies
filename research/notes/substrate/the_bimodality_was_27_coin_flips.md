## The mechanism, closed-form

The exam's PL side is 46 forms, of which **27 (59%) have exactly ONE
training exposure**. Pooled over 12 seeds:

    E=1:   acc 0.546   median own-drive 0.096
    E=2:   acc 0.819
    E=3-5: acc 0.824   median own-drive (E>=2) 0.419
    E>=6:  acc 0.792

A step function at E=2 — precisely where COLT22's Remark-2 margin puts
it at this operating point: one exposure buys boost (1+0.2)^5 ≈ 2.5,
BELOW the n=10⁵ extreme factor √(2·ln(n/k)/kp) ≈ 3.3; two exposures
buy 6.2, above it. The per-seed "bimodality" is nothing but the
sampling variance of 27 near-margin coin flips over a 46-item exam;
per-seed PL accuracy is a binomial artifact of a threshold phenomenon.
SG stays ~1.0 even for its 1-exposure words because its opponent (the
46-assembly PL area) is lightly loaded — the margin is asymmetric in
the imbalanced direction that FAVORS the frequent class under the
paper regime, the mirror image of the label-stimulus era.
