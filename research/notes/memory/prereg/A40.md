tau = n/k / 2, replay is reliable while rho = L k ln n / (n^2 p) <= 0.09, and

### Interpretation, stated now

* A1 to A3 pass: the allocator's rule for one sequence is safe for any split
  of the budget into sequences; past it an area fails per sequence, not per
  area, and short sequences can be loaded further at a known loss -- the cost
  model has a per-sequence reliability term, not only an area-level cliff.
* A1 fails: the safe rule must depend on the split (a short-sequence
  overhead, e.g. the first step from a cue).
* A2 fails: the probe's later failure of short sequences does not carry;
  the budget is a single cliff for any split.
* A3 fails: short sequences fail together in a brain (an area-level event).
* A failed bar is recorded as failed and not moved.

**Reading.** The single-sequence safe rule is safe however the budget is
split: up to rho = 0.09 every sequence of every length replays whole, with a
margin of 23% or more. Past the cliff the two kinds of load fail differently.
One long sequence fails all or none in a brain; many short ones fail one by
one, and every brain loses the same share -- a brain's whole fraction lies
within about 0.03 of the mean, the scatter of independent sequences -- so
the area has no fate of its own: each sequence is a separate trial. Short
sequences therefore outlast the single cliff by 13% to 20% in rho_50, and a
compiler can load an area past the safe rule at a known per-sequence loss.
Under the rule tau = n/k / 2 the one-sequence cliff is at rho_50 = 0.125 at
n/k = 100 and 0.115 at n/k = 171.

**After the run, not tested (post hoc).** A sequence of l elements is whole
with probability c (1 - h)^(l - 1) if the cue captures the first step with
probability c and each later step fails with hazard h. The two lengths give
both: at rho 0.131 to 0.143, h = 0.005 to 0.012 per step and c = 0.39 to
0.91, falling with load -- most short sequences that fail, fail at the cue,
not along the way. One long sequence pays h over thousands of steps, which
is why its failure is a cliff.
